----Problem Statement----
My Parents love gardening, and I have a really big garden on my roof-top. Managing such a large rooftop garden is really hard and requires consistent manual effort. Sometimes they are just busy in office work or sometimes they are not home, because of it plants don’t get water. Resulting in the drying out or even dead plants. Ruining all the efforts of months into a pile of dry leaves.
Upon that my mom used to taunt me for no watering the plants and leaving them dry.

----Solution----
After all the taunts of my mom I decided to water the plants but I’m a really lazy+lethargic person (gym=exception), so I decided to make a autonomous Robotic Car + A Robotic arm attached on top of it system that monitors the soil health and delivers water (via the help of external electric valve) to individual plants with zero human interaction. Saving my Human effort and time.
